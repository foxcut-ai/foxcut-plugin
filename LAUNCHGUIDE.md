# FoxCut

## Summary

Create and manage production-ready AI images and video from ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, Cursor, Grok, and other MCP clients.

## Category

Creative tools; image and video generation; marketing; design.

## Setup

Connect `https://mcp.foxcut.ai/mcp` and complete FoxCut OAuth. A FoxCut account is required. No API key is required.

## Safe first test

Ask: “List FoxCut video models and estimate a five-second 720p video. Do not generate it.”

## Billing

Catalog, account, listing, polling, and estimation tools are read-only. Generation, editing, upscaling, assembly, and character training spend FoxCut credits after the user confirms the estimate.
