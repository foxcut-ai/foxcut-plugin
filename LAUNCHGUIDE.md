# FoxCut

## Summary

Directable AI video and image studio for Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, and other MCP clients. Camera-directed video, text to video, image to video, talking avatars, consistent characters, product shots, and UGC ads. Kling, Sora, Veo, and the FoxCut catalog. Plan credits, then generate, edit, upscale, and assemble.

## Category

Creative tools; AI video generator; text to video; image to video; talking avatars; marketing; design.

## Setup

Connect `https://mcp.foxcut.ai/mcp` and complete FoxCut OAuth. A FoxCut account is required. No API key is required.

## Safe first test

Ask: "List FoxCut video models and estimate a five-second 720p camera-directed video. Do not generate it."

## Billing

Catalog, account, listing, polling, and estimation tools are read-only. Generation, editing, upscaling, assembly, and character training spend FoxCut credits after the user confirms the estimate.
